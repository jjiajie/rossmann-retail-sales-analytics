# 🏪 Rossmann Retail Sales Analytics

A business-focused data analytics project exploring sales patterns across Rossmann stores, with a focus on **weekday behaviour, promotions, holidays, and store performance**.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project originated from a **team-based university project** and was subsequently expanded into an individual retail analytics portfolio project.

The analysis explores Rossmann store sales data to understand how sales performance varies across different operational and calendar factors, and translates these patterns into practical business insights.

## 🎯 Analysis Objectives

The project focuses on several key business questions:

- How does sales performance vary across different days of the week?
- How are promotional periods associated with store sales?
- How do school and state holidays relate to sales patterns?
- How does performance vary across stores?
- What operational insights can be derived from these patterns?

## 🔍 Analysis Workflow

`Retail Sales Data` → `Data Preparation` → `KPI Analysis` → `Weekday Analysis` → `Promotion Analysis` → `Holiday Analysis` → `Store Comparison` → `Business Insights`

## 📊 Key Analysis Areas

### 📅 Weekday Sales Patterns

Analysed average sales across different days of the week to identify recurring patterns in customer demand and store performance.

### 🏷️ Promotion Analysis

Compared sales performance during promotional and non-promotional periods, including matched comparisons across stores and weekdays to reduce differences caused by store scale and weekday composition.

### 🎉 Holiday Analysis

Explored sales behaviour during school holidays and state holidays to identify calendar-related variations in store performance.

### 🏬 Store Performance

Compared store-level performance using business metrics including:

- Average sales
- Total sales
- Customer volume
- Sales per customer

These metrics provide different perspectives on store performance beyond sales revenue alone.

## 💡 Business Applications

The analysis provides insights that may support operational decision-making in areas such as:

- Staffing and workforce planning
- Inventory allocation
- Delivery and replenishment planning
- Promotion planning
- Store performance benchmarking
